How InletCast works

InletCast scores crossing-condition windows for seven North Carolina inlets — Masonboro Inlet, Carolina Beach Inlet, the Cape Fear River entrance, New Topsail Inlet, New River Inlet, Bogue Inlet, and Beaufort Inlet — and pairs them with a Gulf Stream trip planner and live satellite sea-surface temperature for Onslow Bay and the Southeast NC coast.

Privacy

No cookies, no analytics, no tracking. InletCast stores a few things in your browser’s local storage so the site works well: your theme choice, a flag that you dismissed the notice banner, and a cached copy of recent forecast responses (so repeat visits load faster and a dead connection can show the last real forecast instead of nothing). A service worker also caches the app’s own files for offline use. All of it stays on your device, none of it identifies you, and clearing this site’s data in your browser removes it. Forecast data is fetched by your browser directly from Open-Meteo and NOAA, which see your IP address like any web request. Beta-list emails are used only for product updates and are never shared.
